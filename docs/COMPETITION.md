# Competition context

Fluxora targets Topic E, SME Finance Copilot, of the 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition. Its specific contribution is connecting deterministic cash-flow analysis, evidence provenance and constrained preparation of owner-reviewed work. No global uniqueness, selection, eligibility or award is claimed.

Official references, checked 20 October 2026:

- [University announcement](https://ird.oou.cmu.ac.th/2026-shenzhen-international-fintech-competition/)
- [Linked competition guidance](https://drive.scmc.cmu.ac.th/s/2jKErkTJpFmcxYF/download)
- [Competition portal](https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/#/en)

The inspected checklist requests technical documentation, source with README/GitHub/setup/tests, a presentation, security self-assessment and execution evidence. It gives a ten-minute final presentation ceiling and a five-minute video ceiling. One item calls video optional while the non-on-chain evidence item mentions demo video; clarify that discrepancy before relying on omission. PPTX is the project's chosen editable format, not an invented mandated extension.

Registration, student/team eligibility, member documents, repository access and actual entry submission are the entrants' responsibility and remain separate from publishing source. Never add personal registration documents, passports or credentials to this repository. Recheck current organizer instructions before sending. This code performs no registration or competition submission.
