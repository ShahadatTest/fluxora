## Prompt set

The prompts below are the full text submitted to the built-in tool, with the specified transparency flag.

### Logo — transparent background enabled

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production website/app brand symbol for Fluxora, an SME cash-flow action copilot. Create ONE original polished compact flat geometric brand mark, symbol only, NO TEXT and no letters spelled out. A distinctive stylized capital F composed of two flowing interlocking ribbon strokes, expressing direction and clarity, with precise optical balance and a restrained forward/upward cut in its negative space. International premium B2B software identity. Deep forest teal #116754 with a small muted sage #8FB99A accent; predominantly solid teal. Flat vector-like crisp edges, reproducible simple geometry, no gradients, no glass, no shadows, no coins, no bank or currency symbols, no robot, no chart, no enclosing presentation sheet. Single isolated centered symbol fills about 80% of a square canvas, generous clean safe margin. ACTUAL fully transparent background, not a checkerboard illustration. Recognizable at 24px. This is a new original mark, not a variation of a known company's logo.

### Planning illustration — transparent background disabled

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium editorial website illustration for Fluxora, a calm SME cash-flow planning product. Create a wide landscape artwork, roughly 3:2, that represents turning scattered evidence into an organized path forward. Sculptural minimalist 3D still life on a seamless warm off-white #FAFBF8 studio background: three refined ivory paper-like rectangular tiles with simple abstract inset lines, arranged as ascending calm stepping platforms, a single deep forest-teal flowing ribbon passing through the composition and gently curving upward, one restrained muted sage translucent glass arch framing the path. Small subtle warm brass alignment pin near the first platform. Matte ceramic and finely frosted glass, natural soft shadows, precise graceful object spacing. Elegant editorial art direction for an international finance software product, not a game or generic AI template. Center composition with generous clean margins and soft daylight. Palette forest teal #116754, sage #8FB99A, ivory, deep navy accents. No text, words, letters, digits, currency signs, logos, bank buildings, humans, robots, screens, interfaces, fake graphs, financial data or performance claims. Objects should remain architectural and abstract. High finish, restrained materials, simple composition. Flat clean warm off-white backdrop to blend into a light website.

### Evidence illustration — transparent background disabled

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: editorial brand illustration for Fluxora's evidence and owner-review workflow, wide landscape 3:2. Premium understated sculptural 3D still life on seamless warm off-white #FAFBF8, matching a forest teal, sage and ivory B2B cash-flow planning website. Three elegant ivory paper-like document tiles with only abstract engraved lines, one frosted sage glass rectangular pane standing upright, and a continuous deep forest teal ribbon gently linking the tiles through an intentional gap in the glass. A small sculpted teal circular token with a simple check-shaped cutout RESTS BESIDE the last tile, representing a deliberate separate review step. Carefully spaced grounded objects, plausible soft studio daylight and subtle contact shadows, matte ceramic texture and lightly frosted glass. Composition centered, generous negative space around all objects, calm, precise, editorial and architectural, no clutter. NO text, no letters, no numbers, no financial chart or data, no interface, no bank symbols, no currency, no money, no padlock, no shield, no security badge, no claims of certification, no robots, no people, no logos. This is a conceptual decorative illustration, not a picture of a verified or completed financial transaction.
