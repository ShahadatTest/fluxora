# Cash Review

Use forecast for the baseline position, weekly_cashflow for a thirteen-week view, and account_funding when cash must be routed to an account. Use cash_accounts, loans or settlement for their specific recorded positions. Do not replace these tools with mental arithmetic.

Keep each currency separate. Distinguish recorded cash, conditional expected collections, restricted funds and unused noncash credit. A projected low balance is not an observed payment. Source references support inspection, not independent source authentication.

For a changed collection date, require the exact invoice identifier and explicitly supplied date or day shift; use Scenario Comparison for broader alternatives. Do not pick a convenient record. Missing material parameters require clarification.

A requested saved forecast requires the user's exact label and creates only a pending save_forecast workflow. Run no approval, consent, payment or reconciliation step. Financial results and source cards come from deterministic tools after interpretation review, never from your prose.
